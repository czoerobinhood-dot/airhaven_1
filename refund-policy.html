Refund policy

AirHaven follows the same 30-day return terms as our fulfilment partner, InflateFly.

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase from AirHaven. If for any reason you are not satisfied, we offer a hassle-free 30-day return policy. The cost of shipping for returns is the responsibility of the buyer.

Eligibility criteria

Items must be in the same condition as received, with tags attached and in their original packaging. You will need to provide the receipt or proof of purchase.

Initiating a return

To start a return, please contact us at inflateflynt@gmail.com. Returns sent without prior authorization will not be accepted.

Damages and issues

Please inspect your order upon receipt. If you receive a defective, damaged, or incorrect item, contact us immediately so we can resolve the issue promptly.

Exchanges

If you would like a replacement due to a defective, damaged, or incorrect item received, please contact us to arrange a replacement. Additional shipping charges may apply.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will notify you of the approval status. If approved, refunds will be processed within 10 business days to your original payment method. It may take some time for the refund to reflect in your account.

Need assistance?

If you have questions or concerns about this return and refund policy, contact inflatefly@outlook.com. We are here to help.
