Shipping policy

AirHaven provides clear processing, delivery and tracking information for every order.

On this page Shipping time Shipping methods Shipping rates Delivery area Order tracking Lost or damaged items Returns and exchanges Contact us

Thank you for choosing AirHaven. We strive to provide a seamless shopping experience. Please review the shipping terms below.

Shipping time

Orders are typically processed and shipped within 1-3 business days. Delivery times may vary based on your location and chosen shipping method.

Shipping methods

Orders are fulfilled through our delivery network and shipped by established carrier partners to provide reliable and efficient delivery.

Shipping rates

Shipping rates may vary based on order size, weight and delivery location. The applicable rate is shown with your order.

Delivery area

We ship to addresses within the United States, including all 50 states and U.S. territories. We do not currently ship internationally.

Order tracking

Once your order has shipped, you will receive a shipping confirmation with tracking information. Use the tracking number to monitor delivery status.

Lost or damaged items

If your order is lost or damaged during transit, contact AirHaven Customer Service immediately. We will work with the carrier to resolve the issue and arrange a replacement or refund as quickly as possible.

Returns and exchanges

If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, you may return it for a full refund within 30 days of delivery. Please refer to our return and refund policy for return instructions.

Contact us

If you have questions or concerns about your order, contact AirHaven Customer Service. We are available to provide support and address issues you may encounter.
